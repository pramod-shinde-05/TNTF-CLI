Privacy & Security Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Protection

Harborline Bank is committed to protecting your personal and financial information. We use industry-standard AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. All customer information is stored in SOC 2 Type II compliant data centers.

2. Information We Collect

When you open an account, we collect your full name, email address, government-issued identification, and financial details necessary to operate your account. We also collect device and browser information for fraud prevention.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Processing transactions and account management
  • Fraud detection and prevention
  • Compliance with regulatory requirements (KYC/AML)
  • Customer service and account notifications

4. Two-Factor Authentication

We strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) on your account. Harborline supports TOTP authenticator apps and SMS verification for enhanced security.

5. Fraud Monitoring

Our AI-powered fraud detection system monitors all transactions in real-time. If suspicious activity is detected, we may temporarily freeze your account and notify you immediately via email and SMS.

6. Contact Us

For questions about this policy, contact our security team at security@harborlinebank.demo.

Disclaimer: Harborline Bank is a fictional demo application created for network security testing. No real financial services are provided.
